## Project history

| Project | Authors | Repository |
|---|---|---|
| **Gwent Classic** (original) | Arun Sundaram | [asundr/gwent-classic](https://github.com/asundr/gwent-classic) |
| **Gwent Classic v5.0** (fork) | Reynolds Costa, Sylver, Iván Mtz, ia2904 | [ia2904/gwent-classic-v5.0](https://github.com/ia2904/gwent-classic-v5.0) |
| **Gwent Reimagined** (this fork) | grechucia | [grechucia/gwent-reimagined](https://github.com/grechucia/gwent-reimagined) |

Gwent Reimagined is a fork of Gwent Classic v5.0. It replaces the browser front end with a
native Swift + Metal app and adds a career mode, gamepad support and new translations.
The game rules, card data and assets come from Gwent Classic.
